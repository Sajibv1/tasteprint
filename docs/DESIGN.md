# Tasteprint: a personal collection, discovered

## Design contract

The primary visitor is choosing a gift for someone whose cultural interests they know. The main action is **Find their gifts**. Sample walkthroughs let a judge inspect the complete experience before making a live request.

- **Palette:** black `#050505`, charcoal `#111112`, porcelain `#f2f0e9`, stone `#a6a39c`, champagne `#dbc6a0`, iris `#aaa0e5`. Category colors carry graph meaning; champagne marks primary actions.
- **Typography:** Manrope for interface and expressive, tightly set headings. The product name uses the same family. No ornamental metadata or display-font switching.
- **Layout:** a quiet navigation above an asymmetrical, spacious hero. A living 3D taste constellation balances the headline. The gifting workspace follows, with a focused intake, a visible agent activity log, then three curated lanes and a filterable discovery shelf. Editorial photography opens the wider collection below.
- **Signature:** an orbital, fingerprint-like taste constellation. The same graph becomes inspectable during a real run. Animation follows discoveries and user input; the surrounding interface stays calm.
- **Evidence:** show the distinction between stated interests, discovered affinities, and second-hop bridges. A recorded demo is labeled. Product prices are catalog estimates, and cultural imagery is identified as reference artwork.
- **Interaction:** direct form labels, keyboard-operable graph alternatives, persistent saved gifts, actionable empty/error states, cancelable requests, and visible refinement feedback.
- **Responsive:** stack hero and intake below tablet width, wrap controls, use horizontal collection tabs, and keep all essential actions available at 375px. Respect reduced motion and provide a graph view when WebGL is unavailable.

## Review against the brief

The user's requested deep black is the base. A restrained champagne accent and cultural artwork replace a generic neon AI palette. The graph is the one expressive element; identical feature-card grids and decorative gradients do not carry the layout. Collection breadth and a clear interest-to-gift trail demonstrate the product's actual capability.

## Media

Editorial collection images are downloaded from Unsplash and served locally. They illustrate categories rather than claiming to depict the exact recommended product. Album artwork, when present, is sourced from Apple's public iTunes search metadata. Product and cultural reference images are labeled in the interface. Sources are recorded in `public/images/SOURCES.md`.
